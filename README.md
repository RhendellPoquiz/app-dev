 # Weak Hero

**Weak Hero** Because the main character is smart and surprising – He looks weak, but he fights back in clever ways.

## Overview

- **Title:** Weak Hero 
- **Genres:** Action, Drama, School Life, Psychological
- **Author (Webtoon):** SEOPASS (Writer) & RAZEN (Artist)
- **Drama Adaptation Title:** *Weak Hero Class 1*
- **Main Character:** Gray Yeon

##  What Makes It Special?

- **Underdog Hero:** Gray looks small and weak but uses brains and tactics to fight stronger enemies.
- **Real Issues:** Deals with school violence, bullying, and social pressure.
- **Character Growth:** Shows deep friendships and emotional growth.
- **Smart Action:** Fights are more about strategy than brute strength.

##  Main Characters

- **Gray Yeon** – Quiet, intelligent, and deadly when provoked.
- **Sooho Lee** – Loyal friend and strong fighter.
- **Bumseok Yeon** – A troubled student who struggles with identity and jealousy.
